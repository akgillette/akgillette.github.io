Signals worth using

Negative reviews are more diagnostic.

100 words

Typical 1-star review length

1-star reviews average 100 words, compared with 78 for 5-star reviews. Complaints tend to explain the problem in more detail.

82.4%

Overall helpful-vote rate

44,419 of 53,918 helpfulness votes were positive. Low-rated reviews receive more votes per review, making them useful issue-discovery evidence.

68.9%

One-time reviewers

Most customers write a single review. Yet the 31.1% who return account for 55.5% of all reviews.

Reviews per product

Data quality

Use care when modeling at the product level.

10,516

Rows share review text

These duplicated text rows appear under multiple ProductIds, likely reflecting related listings such as product variants, bundle sizes, or mirrored catalog entries.

4.18 → 4.16

Rating result remains stable

The overall mean rating changes only slightly after deduplicating by review text. Descriptive conclusions are robust, even though repeated text affects modeling independence.

Recommendation: for prediction or product comparison, deduplicate review text or use group-aware splits by text/product family. Do not let copies of the same review appear in both training and test sets.

05 / 06
Recommended next actions

Focus improvement efforts where expectations break.

01

Audit listings

Review product photos, size/count statements, bundle definitions, and ingredient details—especially for highly reviewed or poorly rated products.

02

Mine complaints

Use the detailed 1–2 star reviews to tag concrete failure modes: damaged packaging, freshness defects, price frustration, and unmet expectations.

03

Build a robust model

Deduplicate text, use a volume threshold for product comparisons, and retain helpfulness votes as a measure of review usefulness.

The core story is positive product sentiment. The clearest opportunity is operational: make listings and fulfillment reliably match the customer’s expectation.
