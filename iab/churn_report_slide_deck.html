Interpretation

The dataset identifies patterns, not stated reasons

A practical retention hypothesis starts with onboarding, value, and support.
  • SupportedChurn differs sharply by tenure, contract, internet type, support services, charges, and payment method.
  • UnprovenThe file does not show whether customers left because of price, reliability, poor service, or a competitor offer.
  • MissingCancellation reasons, complaints, outages, satisfaction scores, promotions, and competitor data.
Observed associations should guide tests, not causal claims07
Retention agenda

Four tests for the highest-risk customer groups

First-year onboarding

Trigger support check-ins during the first six months and measure incremental retention.

Fiber experience review

Connect churn to outages, speed complaints, installation issues, and local service performance.

Support bundle experiment

Test security and technical support offers among month-to-month internet customers.

Value and contract offer

Test transparent annual pricing or loyalty incentives before using broad discounts.

Measure causal impact with randomized or controlled tests08
